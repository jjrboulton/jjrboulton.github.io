Current Projects & Research

Simulations

My simulations are typically implemented in the Racket programming language. Some areas of exploration include:

  • Custom neural network models
  • Custom symbolic cognitive architectures
  • World modeling, predictive processing, mental simulation, self-modeling, and social cognition
  • The organic emergence of sex, parasitism, symbiosis, retroviral insertion, and more
  • The implementation of cognition with L-system grammars, graph cellular automata, fuzzy logic circuits, and other unconventional artificial cognitive systems

Academic Research

Currently co-authoring a paper with Dr. Douglas Campbell at the University of Canterbury, arguing for a physicalist theory of phenomenal consciousness that blends:

  • a quotational account of phenomenal concepts,
  • a relational account of qualia,
  • a simulation account of social cognition, and
  • an essential indexical account of psychophysical identity statements.

This theory elegantly handles:

  • Kripkean and Chalmersian conceivability arguments,
  • the inverted spectrum argument,
  • Nagel's bat,
  • the explanatory gap,
  • the epistemic gap, and
  • the hard problem of consciousness.
